# Project 4 Documentation --- Data Visualization and Storytelling

## What the project required

Project 4 focused on **data visualization and explanatory
storytelling**. The requirement was to create appropriate charts such as
bar, line, and other suitable visual forms, choose visuals that match
the analytical question, and highlight key insights clearly for a
stakeholder or executive audience rather than simply displaying raw
analysis.

## What I did

I used the findings and PivotTable summaries from the e-commerce dataset
to create a set of business-focused Excel visualizations.

-   Created a monthly order-value trend chart to show performance over
    time.
-   Visualized product performance to compare each product's
    contribution to recorded order value.
-   Presented order outcomes across Cancelled, Delivered, Pending,
    Returned, and Shipped statuses.
-   Visualized customer acquisition channels by order count and recorded
    order value.
-   Presented promotional offer usage across FREESHIP, No Coupon,
    SAVE10, and WINTER15.
-   Compared payment channels including Cash, Credit Card, Debit Card,
    Gift Card, and Online.
-   Produced a collection consisting of a line chart, doughnut chart,
    box-plot/outlier work, and multiple bar charts, and created an
    executive-dashboard navigation layout for the workbook.

## Main messages communicated

-   The dataset contains **\$1.265 million in total recorded order
    value** across **1,200 orders**.
-   Chairs and Printers are the leading products by recorded order
    value.
-   Instagram is the leading customer acquisition channel by both order
    count and recorded order value.
-   Cancelled and Returned orders together represent approximately
    **41.4%** of all order records, making order outcomes an important
    management attention area.
-   Promotional-offer usage is relatively distributed, with FREESHIP
    used most frequently.
-   Online has the highest number of orders among payment methods, while
    Credit Card has the highest recorded order value.

## Outcome

The project transformed the exploratory analysis into a clearer
stakeholder-facing story by prioritizing trends, comparisons, and
operational outcomes. The visual set is designed to help a viewer
understand the most important findings quickly and connect them to
business attention areas.
